Your private transfer

Travel privately from Nosara to Playa Conchal with Travesia Costa Rica. Playa Conchal is a stunning shell-covered beach in Guanacaste, home to luxury resorts like the Westin Reserva Conchal. The transfer takes about 2h, door to door, with a professional local driver. One flat price per vehicle - taxes and tolls included, and no per-person fees.

What to expect on the Nosara → Playa Conchal drive

The private transfer from Nosara to Playa Conchal takes about 2h, and your driver takes you straight there — no transfers, and no waiting on other passengers. Prefer to break up the trip? Quick courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee or a photo are included — just ask your driver, and on the longer routes they can point out a scenic spot to stretch your legs. We track your flight, so a delay is never a problem, and free child seats are ready on request. Planning the rest of your trip? Browse our Costa Rica travel guides.

What you’ll see between Nosara and Playa Conchal

The road between Nosara and Playa Conchal goes into the Nicoya Peninsula through Santa Cruz and Nicoya. For Sámara and Nosara you drop down to the coast from there; for Santa Teresa you cross the whole peninsula through Jicaral and Cóbano.

  • Nicoya, one of the oldest towns in Costa Rica and the heart of the Blue Zone
  • Dry hills and cattle pasture - a very different Costa Rica from the green of the north
  • Howler monkeys in the roadside trees, especially early in the morning
  • The drop down into Sámara's calm, reef-protected bay

Road conditions

Paved as far as Nicoya and Sámara. The last stretches into Nosara and Santa Teresa are still stone and gravel road, so your driver takes them slowly.

From your driver

This part of the country is slower than the map suggests - it is curves and small towns, not highway. Leaving early changes the whole trip.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from Nosara to Playa Conchal?
The private shuttle takes about 2h door to door, depending on traffic and any stops you request.
How much is a shuttle from Nosara to Playa Conchal?
From $235 per vehicle for up to 5 passengers (Hyundai Staria), with larger vehicles available. The price is per vehicle, not per person, and includes taxes and tolls.
Is the transfer private?
Yes. The vehicle is exclusively for you and your group - no strangers and no extra stops. Door-to-door, hotel to hotel.
What if my flight is delayed?
Just share your flight number when you book. We monitor it and adjust your pickup time at no extra cost.
Do you pick up at any address in Nosara?
Yes. The transfer is door to door: we collect you at your hotel, villa, Airbnb or airport terminal in Nosara and drop you right at the door of where you are staying in Playa Conchal.
How many passengers fit in the vehicle?
Up to 5 in the Hyundai Staria, up to 9 in the Toyota Hiace and up to 12 in the Maxus V90 - always with room for the luggage. Tell us how many bags you are carrying and we assign the right vehicle.
Are child seats included?
Yes, and they are free. Tell us the ages of the children when you book and the seats are installed and ready in the vehicle.
Can we stop along the way?
Yes. Courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee, food or a photo are included on every transfer - just ask your driver. If you want a guided stop at a real attraction on the way, the VIP upgrade adds a guide, tourist stops and a welcome kit for $80 more.
How do I pay?
You can book and pay by card online on our secure payment page, or arrange it with us on WhatsApp. Payment is in full at booking, and the price you see is the final price - taxes and tolls included, with no hidden fees.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your pickup time. Within 48 hours of the pickup the booking is non-refundable.
