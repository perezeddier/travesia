Your private transfer

Travel privately from Papagayo to Playas del Coco with Travesia Costa Rica. Playas del Coco is a lively Guanacaste beach town close to Liberia Airport, great for diving and dining. The transfer takes about 30min, door to door, with a professional local driver. One flat price per vehicle - taxes and tolls included, and no per-person fees.

What to expect on the Papagayo → Playas del Coco drive

The private transfer from Papagayo to Playas del Coco takes about 30min, and your driver takes you straight there — no transfers, and no waiting on other passengers. Prefer to break up the trip? Quick courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee or a photo are included — just ask your driver, and on the longer routes they can point out a scenic spot to stretch your legs. We track your flight, so a delay is never a problem, and free child seats are ready on request. Planning the rest of your trip? Browse our Costa Rica travel guides.

What you’ll see between Papagayo and Playas del Coco

The drive between Papagayo and Playas del Coco stays inside Guanacaste, on the short coastal roads through Comunidad, Sardinal, Filadelfia and the junctions at Huacas and Villarreal.

  • The dry tropical savanna, with Brahman cattle grazing right by the road
  • Guanacaste trees, and the yellow cortez in bloom in the dry season
  • Filadelfia and Santa Cruz, working sabanero towns rather than resort strips
  • The Gulf of Papagayo opening up as you come down to the coast

Road conditions

Paved the whole way - the roads down to these beaches are surfaced now, not gravel.

From your driver

These are short hops - twenty minutes to about an hour - so there is room to stop and eat without missing your check-in.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from Papagayo to Playas del Coco?
The private shuttle takes about 30min door to door, depending on traffic and any stops you request.
How much is a shuttle from Papagayo to Playas del Coco?
From $120 per vehicle for up to 5 passengers (Hyundai Staria), with larger vehicles available. The price is per vehicle, not per person, and includes taxes and tolls.
Is the transfer private?
Yes. The vehicle is exclusively for you and your group - no strangers and no extra stops. Door-to-door, hotel to hotel.
What if my flight is delayed?
Just share your flight number when you book. We monitor it and adjust your pickup time at no extra cost.
Do you pick up at any address in Papagayo?
Yes. The transfer is door to door: we collect you at your hotel, villa, Airbnb or airport terminal in Papagayo and drop you right at the door of where you are staying in Playas del Coco.
How many passengers fit in the vehicle?
Up to 5 in the Hyundai Staria, up to 9 in the Toyota Hiace and up to 12 in the Maxus V90 - always with room for the luggage. Tell us how many bags you are carrying and we assign the right vehicle.
Are child seats included?
Yes, and they are free. Tell us the ages of the children when you book and the seats are installed and ready in the vehicle.
Can we stop along the way?
Yes. Courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee, food or a photo are included on every transfer - just ask your driver. If you want a guided stop at a real attraction on the way, the VIP upgrade adds a guide, tourist stops and a welcome kit for $80 more.
How do I pay?
You can book and pay by card online on our secure payment page, or arrange it with us on WhatsApp. Payment is in full at booking, and the price you see is the final price - taxes and tolls included, with no hidden fees.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your pickup time. Within 48 hours of the pickup the booking is non-refundable.
