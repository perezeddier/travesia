Your private transfer

Travel privately from San Jose (city) to Playas del Coco with Travesia Costa Rica. Playas del Coco is a lively Guanacaste beach town close to Liberia Airport, great for diving and dining. The transfer takes about 5h, door to door, with a professional local driver. One flat price per vehicle - taxes and tolls included, and no per-person fees.

What to expect on the San Jose (city) → Playas del Coco drive

The private transfer from San Jose (city) to Playas del Coco takes about 5h, and your driver takes you straight there — no transfers, and no waiting on other passengers. Prefer to break up the trip? Quick courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee or a photo are included — just ask your driver, and on the longer routes they can point out a scenic spot to stretch your legs. We track your flight, so a delay is never a problem, and free child seats are ready on request. Planning the rest of your trip? Browse our Costa Rica travel guides.

What you’ll see between San Jose (city) and Playas del Coco

Between San Jose (city) and Playas del Coco we cross the country from the Central Valley out to Guanacaste. Depending on how the traffic is running that day we take the Pan-American (Route 1) through Esparza and Cañas, or Route 27 down to Caldera and north from there.

  • The descent out of the Central Valley - cool mountain air to Guanacaste heat in about an hour
  • The Gulf of Nicoya on your left around Esparza
  • Sugar cane and rice fields through Cañas and Bagaces
  • The Miravalles and Rincón de la Vieja volcanoes standing on the right
  • Liberia, the white city, before you turn off for the coast

Road conditions

Paved from end to end and one of the easiest long drives in the country, with long straight stretches.

From your driver

It is around five hours on the road, so your driver plans a proper stop along the way for the restroom and something to eat.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from San Jose (city) to Playas del Coco?
The private shuttle takes about 5h door to door, depending on traffic and any stops you request.
How much is a shuttle from San Jose (city) to Playas del Coco?
From $360 per vehicle for up to 5 passengers (Hyundai Staria), with larger vehicles available. The price is per vehicle, not per person, and includes taxes and tolls.
Is the transfer private?
Yes. The vehicle is exclusively for you and your group - no strangers and no extra stops. Door-to-door, hotel to hotel.
What if my flight is delayed?
Just share your flight number when you book. We monitor it and adjust your pickup time at no extra cost.
Do you pick up at any address in San Jose (city)?
Yes. The transfer is door to door: we collect you at your hotel, villa, Airbnb or airport terminal in San Jose (city) and drop you right at the door of where you are staying in Playas del Coco.
How many passengers fit in the vehicle?
Up to 5 in the Hyundai Staria, up to 9 in the Toyota Hiace and up to 12 in the Maxus V90 - always with room for the luggage. Tell us how many bags you are carrying and we assign the right vehicle.
Are child seats included?
Yes, and they are free. Tell us the ages of the children when you book and the seats are installed and ready in the vehicle.
Can we stop along the way?
Yes. Courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee, food or a photo are included on every transfer - just ask your driver. If you want a guided stop at a real attraction on the way, the VIP upgrade adds a guide, tourist stops and a welcome kit for $80 more.
How do I pay?
You can book and pay by card online on our secure payment page, or arrange it with us on WhatsApp. Payment is in full at booking, and the price you see is the final price - taxes and tolls included, with no hidden fees.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your pickup time. Within 48 hours of the pickup the booking is non-refundable.
