Your private transfer

Travel privately from Liberia Airport (LIR) to La Fortuna / Arenal with Travesia Costa Rica. La Fortuna is the gateway to the Arenal Volcano, hot springs, waterfalls and hanging bridges - one of Costa Rica's most popular destinations. The transfer takes about 3h, door to door, with a professional local driver. One flat price per vehicle - taxes and tolls included, and no per-person fees.

What to expect on the Liberia Airport (LIR) → La Fortuna / Arenal drive

The private transfer from Liberia Airport (LIR) to La Fortuna / Arenal takes about 3h, and your driver takes you straight there — no transfers, and no waiting on other passengers. Prefer to break up the trip? Quick courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee or a photo are included — just ask your driver, and on the longer routes they can point out a scenic spot to stretch your legs. We track your flight, so a delay is never a problem, and free child seats are ready on request. Planning the rest of your trip? Browse our Costa Rica travel guides.

What you’ll see between Liberia Airport (LIR) and La Fortuna / Arenal

Between Liberia Airport (LIR) and La Fortuna / Arenal we normally go around Lake Arenal through Tilarán and come out at Cañas. On some days we take the Bijagua road instead, on the flank of Tenorio volcano.

  • Arenal volcano in the mirror for the first twenty minutes
  • The whole of Lake Arenal, with the wind turbines lined up on the ridge
  • Tilarán, a windy cattle town above the lake
  • The switch from rainforest to dry savanna in under an hour

Road conditions

Paved for most of the way - there are some unpaved stretches along the lake - and one curve after another. What costs time here is the bends, not the distance.

From your driver

If we go around the lake, the stop is Café y Macadamia on the shore: fresh food and one of the best views of the drive.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from Liberia Airport (LIR) to La Fortuna / Arenal?
The private shuttle takes about 3h door to door, depending on traffic and any stops you request.
How much is a shuttle from Liberia Airport (LIR) to La Fortuna / Arenal?
From $225 per vehicle for up to 5 passengers (Hyundai Staria), with larger vehicles available. The price is per vehicle, not per person, and includes taxes and tolls.
Is the transfer private?
Yes. The vehicle is exclusively for you and your group - no strangers and no extra stops. Door-to-door, hotel to hotel.
What if my flight is delayed?
Just share your flight number when you book. We monitor it and adjust your pickup time at no extra cost.
Do you pick up at any address in Liberia Airport (LIR)?
Yes. The transfer is door to door: we collect you at your hotel, villa, Airbnb or airport terminal in Liberia Airport (LIR) and drop you right at the door of where you are staying in La Fortuna / Arenal.
How many passengers fit in the vehicle?
Up to 5 in the Hyundai Staria, up to 9 in the Toyota Hiace and up to 12 in the Maxus V90 - always with room for the luggage. Tell us how many bags you are carrying and we assign the right vehicle.
Are child seats included?
Yes, and they are free. Tell us the ages of the children when you book and the seats are installed and ready in the vehicle.
Can we stop along the way?
Yes. Courtesy stops for the restroom, a coffee, food or a photo are included on every transfer - just ask your driver. If you want a guided stop at a real attraction on the way, the VIP upgrade adds a guide, tourist stops and a welcome kit for $80 more.
How do I pay?
You can book and pay by card online on our secure payment page, or arrange it with us on WhatsApp. Payment is in full at booking, and the price you see is the final price - taxes and tolls included, with no hidden fees.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your pickup time. Within 48 hours of the pickup the booking is non-refundable.
