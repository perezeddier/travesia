Frequently asked questions

What's the minimum number of days?
2 consecutive days. There's no maximum — book as many days as your trip needs.
Does the daily rate change if we travel farther that day?
No. It's a flat daily rate per vehicle (taxes included) for a reasonable daily itinerary, whether you explore one area or cover more ground that day.
Does the price include fuel and tolls?
Yes. The vehicle, professional driver, fuel and road tolls are all included in the daily rate.
Can we go somewhere different each day?
Yes. Tell your driver your plan each morning or the night before — it's flexible, not a fixed itinerary.
Can we add the Travesía VIP upgrade?
Yes. Travesía VIP (+$80, one-time) adds a guaranteed bilingual guide and curated tourist stops for your whole trip.
