Caño Negro Wildlife Boat Safari

~7 hours $96.05 / person Easy · lunch included

Costa Rica's greatest wetland, by boat

The Caño Negro wetlands, near the Nicaraguan border, are one of the most important wildlife refuges in Central America — and the best way to see them is exactly how you will: gliding along the Río Frío by boat. This is paradise for animal lovers and bird watchers: more than 300 bird species, capuchin monkeys, caimans, sloths, iguanas and more, all spotted from the comfort of your seat.

Your experience

  1. Hotel pickup in the La Fortuna / Arenal area and a scenic drive through northern farmland.
  2. ~2-hour boat safari on the Río Frío through the Caño Negro refuge — cameras and binoculars ready.
  3. Local lunch at a riverside restaurant.
  4. Sugar cane & chocolate tasting — a sweet look at two of Costa Rica's classic crops.
  5. Return to your hotel.

What’s included

  • Hotel pickup & transport
  • Boat safari with life jackets
  • Expert bilingual guide
  • Lunch at a local restaurant
  • Sugar cane & chocolate tasting
  • Fruits & water refills
