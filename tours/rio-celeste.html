Río Celeste Waterfall & Volcano Trek

~5–6 hours $140 / person Lunch & entrance included

The river that turns turquoise

Deep inside Tenorio Volcano National Park, two ordinary rivers meet — and the water suddenly turns a brilliant, unreal turquoise blue. It's one of Costa Rica's most jaw-dropping natural sights, and this full-day tour takes you right to it: the famous 30-meter Río Celeste waterfall, the blue lagoon, bubbling volcanic mud pots, and Los Teñideros — the exact spot where the color is born.

Your day

  1. Hotel pickup in the La Fortuna / Arenal area and a scenic drive to the base of Tenorio Volcano.
  2. Guided rainforest hike to the Río Celeste waterfall and blue lagoon (moderate — some sections are uphill).
  3. Volcanic hot spots — bubbling mud pots and steaming vents that give the river its color.
  4. Los Teñideros — witness the two rivers merge into that impossible turquoise.
  5. Traditional lunch before the drive back to your hotel.

What’s included

  • Air-conditioned transport & pickup
  • National park entrance fee
  • Certified bilingual guide
  • Traditional Costa Rican lunch
  • Water refills (bring your bottle)
