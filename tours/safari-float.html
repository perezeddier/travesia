Safari Float Wildlife River Tour

~4.5 hours $83.06 / person Easy · all ages

Costa Rica's wildlife, from the calmest seat in the house

No rapids, no paddling stress — just a slow, silent drift down the Peñas Blancas River while your guide points out the animals most visitors walk right past: sloths hanging overhead, monkeys in the branches, crocodiles on the banks and dozens of tropical birds. It's the best wildlife-per-minute experience around La Fortuna, and because the raft does the work, it's perfect for all ages — from toddlers to grandparents.

Your experience

  1. Hotel pickup in the La Fortuna / Arenal area.
  2. Gentle float down the Peñas Blancas River with about two hours of quiet wildlife spotting alongside an expert bilingual guide.
  3. Jungle farm stop — recharge with fresh tropical fruits and artisan coffee, and learn how coffee, chocolate and sugar cane are grown.
  4. Return to your hotel.

What’s included

  • Hotel pickup & transport
  • Expert bilingual guide
  • Raft & life jacket
  • Costa Rican snack
  • Tropical fruits & coffee at the farm
  • Water refills (bring your bottle)
