Hanging Bridges + La Fortuna Waterfall

Combo day $160 / person Lunch included

Treetops in the morning, waterfall in the afternoon

Two of La Fortuna's favorites in one well-rounded day. Start high in the canopy on the Místico hanging bridges, spotting wildlife with an expert guide and soaking up open views of the Arenal Volcano. Break for a traditional Costa Rican lunch, then cool off with a swim beneath the La Fortuna Waterfall — a perfect blend of easy adventure, nature and reward.

Your day

  1. Hotel pickup in the La Fortuna / Arenal area.
  2. Hanging bridges walk — guided canopy walk across the Místico bridge network with wildlife spotting and volcano views.
  3. Traditional lunch — a hearty local meal at a restaurant.
  4. La Fortuna Waterfall — descend to the base and swim in the crystal-clear pools.
  5. Return to your hotel.

What’s included

  • Round-trip transport & pickup
  • Certified bilingual guide
  • Hanging bridges park entrance
  • Waterfall entrance
  • Traditional lunch
  • Water refills (bring your bottle)

Price: $160 per person · taxes included · kids 4–11 pay half · under 4 free · moderate difficulty · free cancellation up to 48h.
