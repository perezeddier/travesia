La Fortuna Full-Day Adventure

Full day (~8h) From $192 / person Private group · min. 4 people

One day, all the best of Arenal

Make the most of your time in La Fortuna with a private, full-day tour that brings together the area's top experiences — nature, adventure, culture and food — with your own private transport and guide. Perfect for families and groups of friends (minimum 4 people).

Your day

  1. Mistico Hanging Bridges — walk among the treetops of the Arenal rainforest across a network of hanging bridges, with a chance to spot wildlife.
  2. Volcano hike — El Silencio Reserve — trek across old lava fields with stunning views of the Arenal Volcano.
  3. Tortilla making — a hands-on local experience making traditional Costa Rican tortillas.
  4. Traditional lunch — a delicious typical meal to recharge.
  5. La Fortuna Waterfall — finish at one of Costa Rica's most beautiful waterfalls.

What’s included

  • Private transport all day
  • Private guide
  • Mistico Hanging Bridges
  • Volcano hike (El Silencio)
  • Tortilla-making experience
  • Traditional lunch
  • La Fortuna Waterfall

Price: $192 per person · taxes included · minimum 4 people.
