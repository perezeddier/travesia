Canyoning & Waterfall Rappel

5 rappels · 2 ziplines $105 / person Ages 5+ · no experience needed

Rappel down real jungle waterfalls

This is the adventure people talk about for years: descending a rainforest canyon near La Fortuna on 5 rappels down 3 living waterfalls, water rushing past you, jungle all around. The rappels build up gradually — you start small, gain confidence, and finish on the big one: a drop of roughly 60 meters. Between rappels, two ziplines (~160m and ~400m) fly you across the canyon.

No experience needed — professional canyoning guides handle your ropes and safety at every step, and kids from age 5 can join. You'll earn that Costa Rican lunch at the end.

Your experience

  1. Pickup / meeting in the La Fortuna area and safety briefing with full gear fitting.
  2. Canyon descent — 5 rappels down 3 waterfalls, from starter drops to the ~60m finale.
  3. Ziplines — two canyon crossings (~160m and ~400m).
  4. Costa Rican lunch to close the adventure.

What’s included

  • Transport from the La Fortuna area
  • Professional canyoning guides
  • All climbing & safety equipment
  • 5 rappels · 3 waterfalls · 2 ziplines
  • Costa Rican lunch
