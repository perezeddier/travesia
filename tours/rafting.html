White Water Rafting

~5 hours From $70 / person Lunch & gear included

The Balsa River, two ways to ride it

Rafting is La Fortuna's classic adrenaline fix, and the Balsa River — fed by the Arenal watershed and wrapped in rainforest — is the perfect place for it. Choose your level:

Option 1 — Class II–III · family fun · $70

The best-seller. Lively, splashy rapids with calm stretches in between to watch for wildlife on the banks. Ages 6 and up — perfect for families and first-timers who want real rapids without the fear factor. ~5 hours door to door.

Option 2 — Class III–IV · adrenaline · $74

The Balsa's upper section: bigger drops, faster water, more paddling — a proper adventure for travelers who want their heart rate up. Ages 12 and up. ~5 hours door to door.

Both options include

  • Hotel pickup & transport
  • Professional river guides
  • All safety gear (helmet, vest, paddle)
  • Typical Costa Rican lunch
  • Fresh fruit on the river
  • Towels

Prices per person · taxes included · Class II–III from age 6 · Class III–IV from age 12 · free cancellation up to 48h.
