The person behind the company

Travesía is me, Eddie — a local driver based in La Fortuna. From 2011 to 2018 I drove travelers all over Costa Rica for two established tourism companies, learning the roads, the destinations and the details that make a trip easier. I then spent a few years as a teacher, which taught me the patience and care my guests still notice today. In 2022 I put it all together and founded Travesía.

When you book, you are not talking to a call center — you are talking directly with me on WhatsApp. I make sure every trip across Costa Rica is safe, comfortable and stress-free. Pura vida!

Why that matters on the road

  • The person who answers your message is the person responsible for your trip
  • We drive these corridors ourselves, so the advice you get is first-hand, not from a script
  • Small company, so a late flight or a change of plan is handled by one phone call, not a ticket queue
  • Every driver on the team is local, bilingual and known to us personally
