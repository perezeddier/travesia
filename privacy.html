Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This page explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect when you book with Travesía Costa Rica, why we need it, and the choices you have. Last updated: August 2026.

Travesía Costa Rica ("Travesía", "we", "us") is a private ground-transportation and day-tour business based in La Fortuna, Costa Rica. We only collect the information we genuinely need to plan your trip and stay in touch with you about it. We do not sell your data — ever.

1. Information we collect

When you request a quote or make a booking, you share details with us so we can plan your transfer. Depending on how you contact us, this may include:

  • Contact details — your name, WhatsApp or phone number, and email address.
  • Trip details — pickup and drop-off addresses, travel dates and times, number of passengers, children's ages (so we prepare the right child seats), and any special requests.
  • Flight information — your flight number for airport pickups, so we can track arrivals and adjust your pickup.
  • Messages — the content of the messages you send us by WhatsApp, email or our website.

We do not collect or store your card or bank details on this website. When online card payment becomes available, it will be handled by a specialized, secure payment provider — your card data goes to them, not to us.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to run the service you asked for:

  • To prepare your quote and confirm your booking.
  • To plan your route, assign the right vehicle and driver, and prepare child seats or special requests.
  • To track your flight and coordinate your airport pickup.
  • To reconfirm details and share your driver's information before your trip.
  • To stay in touch with you about your reservation and answer your questions.

3. How we contact you

We mainly communicate through WhatsApp, and sometimes by email or phone. We message you about your own booking and quote — we do not send marketing spam. If you'd rather we reach you a specific way, just tell us.

4. Who we share it with

We keep your information within our small team and only share what's necessary to deliver your trip. This means:

  • Your driver — receives the details needed to pick you up (name, pickup location, time, flight, contact number).
  • Service providers we rely on — such as WhatsApp (Meta) for messaging, our website host, and, when applicable, a secure payment provider.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Cookies & website tools

Our website is a simple, static site. It uses a small amount of your browser's local storage to remember your language choice and the trip you're building — this stays on your device. We also load a few trusted third-party tools that may set cookies or receive basic technical data (like your IP address) to work:

  • Google Fonts — to display the site's typography.
  • Google & TripAdvisor reviews widgets — to show our real customer reviews.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your booking information for as long as needed to provide the service and to keep reasonable business records. When it's no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete your details at any time (see section 8).

7. How we protect it

Our website is served over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS). We limit access to your information to the people who need it to run your trip, and we take reasonable steps to keep it safe. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we treat your data with care.

8. Your rights

Your information is yours. You can ask us to:

  • See what information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information when it's no longer needed for your booking.

Just message us and we'll take care of it.

9. Children's privacy

Our service is booked by adults. We only collect children's ages when a parent or guardian provides them so we can prepare the correct child seats — nothing more.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always here on this page, with the "last updated" date at the top.

11. Contact us

Questions about your privacy or the information we hold? We're one message away.
