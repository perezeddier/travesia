What is a private shuttle in Costa Rica?

A private shuttle is a vehicle booked exclusively for your group, with a professional driver, that takes you door to door between any two points in Costa Rica — airport to hotel, hotel to hotel, beach to volcano. There are no other passengers, no fixed departure time, and no stops you didn't ask for.

It's the most common way visitors get around Costa Rica without renting a car, because the country's distances are longer than they look on a map and the mountain roads take real local experience to drive well.

Frequently asked questions

What is a private shuttle in Costa Rica?
A private shuttle is a vehicle booked exclusively for your group, with a professional driver, that takes you door to door between any two points in Costa Rica. There are no other passengers, no fixed departure schedule and no extra stops you did not ask for.
How much does a private shuttle cost in Costa Rica?
The price is per vehicle, not per person. With Travesía it starts at $135 for short routes such as Liberia Airport to Tamarindo, and is $220 for San José Airport to La Fortuna in a vehicle for up to 5 passengers. Taxes and tolls are always included.
Is a private shuttle better than a shared shuttle?
A shared shuttle is cheaper for one or two people, but it runs on a fixed schedule and stops at several hotels along the way. A private shuttle leaves when you want, goes straight to your destination and is usually cheaper than a shared shuttle once you are three or more people, because you pay per vehicle.
What if my flight is delayed?
Share your flight number when you book and we track it. If your flight is late we adjust the pickup time at no extra cost.
How many passengers fit in a private shuttle?
We have three vehicle sizes: Hyundai Staria for up to 5 passengers, Toyota Hiace for up to 9, and Maxus V90 for up to 12. For larger groups we combine vehicles.
Can I book a private shuttle with several stops or several days?
Yes. You can chain several transfers in one booking, and for trips of two or more consecutive days you can book a dedicated driver and vehicle with our Full Trip Chauffeur service.
