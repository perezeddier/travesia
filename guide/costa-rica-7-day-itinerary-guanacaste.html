Costa Rica 7-Day Itinerary: Arenal & Guanacaste Beaches

Volcano first, then sun and surf. Perfect if you fly into Liberia (LIR) and want to mix the Arenal rainforest with the golden beaches of Guanacaste around Tamarindo — with hotels for every budget, tours, restaurants and your transport planned out.

This route pairs Costa Rica's most iconic volcano with its most popular beach region. Fly into Liberia (LIR), spend three nights at Arenal / La Fortuna, then four nights on the Guanacaste coast around Tamarindo before flying home from Liberia. Two hotel options at each stop — budget 💵 and 5-star ✨ — plus tours, restaurants and the exact private transfers.

Prices are per vehicle, not per person — up to 5 passengers in a Hyundai Staria; larger vans available. Taxes and tolls included.

Days 1–3 · Arenal / La Fortuna 🌋

Your driver meets you at Liberia Airport and takes you to La Fortuna (about 3 hours). Three nights of waterfalls, hot springs and volcano views.

Where to stay

  • 💵 Budget: Arenal Backpackers Resort, Hotel Arenal Rossi, Selina La Fortuna, Roca Verde, Hotel Las Colinas.
  • ✨ 5-star: Nayara Springs, Nayara Gardens, Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa, The Springs Resort & Spa, Arenal Kioro Suites & Spa.

Best things to do

⭐ Our pick — the Travesía La Fortuna Full-Day Tour: Mistico hanging bridges, a guided volcano hike at El Silencio, tortilla-making, a traditional lunch and the La Fortuna Waterfall — with private transport and a private guide. See the tour →
  • El Silencio volcano trail — our recommended hike, with up-close Arenal views.
  • La Fortuna Waterfall & a dip in the pool below.
  • Mistico Arenal Hanging Bridges through the canopy.
  • Hot springs at Tabacón or Baldí.
  • Optional day trip to the turquoise Río Celeste waterfall.

Where to eat

  • Don Rufino — lively local favourite, great steaks & casados.
  • Soda La Parada — classic, affordable Tico food in town.
  • Órganico Fortuna — fresh bowls, smoothies & vegetarian options.
  • Chifa La Familia Feliz — popular Peruvian–Chinese.
  • Anch'io Ristorante — wood-fired pizza & Italian.
🚐 Getting here: Liberia (LIR) → La Fortuna, ~3h, from $225 per vehicle. See price & book this transfer →

Days 4–7 · Tamarindo & the Guanacaste beaches 🏄

Cross to the Pacific coast (about 4.5 hours) for four nights of beaches, sunsets and surf. Tamarindo is a lively hub with easy access to Playa Conchal, Flamingo and Playa Grande.

Where to stay

  • 💵 Budget: Tamarindo Backpackers, Hotel Pasatiempo, La Botella de Leche, Pura Vida Mini Hostel, Blue Trailz Hotel.
  • ✨ 5-star: Hotel Capitán Suizo, Wyndham Tamarindo, Westin Reserva Conchal, W Costa Rica, Tamarindo Diriá Beach Resort.

Best things to do

  • Sunset catamaran sail with snorkeling.
  • Surf lessons on Tamarindo's beginner-friendly waves.
  • Day trip to Rincón de la Vieja volcano — waterfalls, hot springs & zip-lines.
  • Beach-hopping to Playa Conchal & Flamingo.
  • Palo Verde boat safari, or turtle nesting at Playa Grande (seasonal).

Where to eat

  • Pangas Beach Club — romantic dinner on the sand.
  • Nogui's — Tamarindo classic on the beach.
  • Green Papaya Taco Bar — fresh, casual tacos & bowls.
  • El Mercadito — open-air food hall, great value.
  • Dragonfly Bar & Grill — long-time local favourite for dinner.
🚐 Getting here: La Fortuna → Tamarindo, ~4.5h, from $315 per vehicle. See price & book this transfer →
