Landing at SJO: Your Arrival Guide

From the plane to your hotel, step by step — immigration, customs, SIM cards, money, and how to get out of the airport without stress. Written by drivers who pick travelers up at SJO every week.

Step 1 — Immigration

Follow the crowd from the gate to immigration. You'll need your passport and may be asked for proof of onward travel (your return ticket on your phone is fine). Lines take 15–60 minutes — early-morning and evening arrivals are usually quick; midday, when several US flights land together, is the slow window. The officer typically stamps you in for up to 180 days.

Step 2 — Baggage claim & customs

Baggage claim is right after immigration (carts are free). Customs is refreshingly simple: your bags go through a scanner on the way out, and that's it for most travelers. Nothing to declare? You're through in a minute.

Step 3 — SIM card, ATM, restrooms (optional, 5 minutes)

  • SIM cards: kiosks in and just past the baggage/customs area sell local prepaid SIMs (Kölbi, Claro) for a few dollars with plenty of data. If you set up an eSIM before flying, you can skip this entirely.
  • Money: you don't need colones on day one — US dollars in small bills work everywhere, and there are ATMs at the airport if you want local cash. Skip the currency-exchange counters (poor rates).
  • Restrooms: use them before exiting — your next stop may be a 3-hour scenic drive.

Step 4 — The exit walkway (know this part!)

When you step out of customs you enter a covered outdoor walkway with a railing and a lot of people: families, tour reps, official drivers with name signs — and unofficial "taxi" drivers who will approach you offering rides. They're not regulated, often charge double, and this is where most arrival scams happen. The rule is simple: never go with someone who approaches you. Walk with purpose to the transport you've already arranged.

How far is everything from SJO?

Surprise: SJO isn't in San José — it's in Alajuela, about 20–30 minutes from downtown. Real-world drive times from the airport: La Fortuna / Arenal ~3 h · Manuel Antonio ~3 h · Monteverde ~3 h · Jacó ~1.5 h · Puerto Viejo (Caribbean) ~4.5 h. If you land after 3 pm and your destination is 3+ hours away, consider a night near the airport and a fresh morning transfer — rural roads are best enjoyed in daylight.

Landing late at night?

SJO runs late arrivals daily and official taxis operate 24h. If you've pre-booked a transfer, your driver is tracking the flight — a delay costs you nothing. Book a hotel with 24h reception, keep your first night simple, and start the adventure rested.
