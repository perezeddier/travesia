Best Restaurants in Costa Rica: La Fortuna, San José, Monteverde & Manuel Antonio

Where to eat in Costa Rica's most-visited stops — from budget sodas to special-occasion dining. Here are our favourites in each town, with a quick note on what to order.

Costa Rican food is fresh, friendly and great value. A local soda (family diner) serves a hearty casado — rice, beans, plantain, salad and your choice of meat or fish — for a few dollars, while the top destinations also have excellent international and fine dining. Prices below are a rough guide: 💵 budget · 💰 mid-range · ✨ special occasion.

🌋 La Fortuna / Arenal

  • 💰 Don Rufino — the town's most popular table; excellent steaks, casados and cocktails.
  • ✨ Anch'io Ristorante — wood-fired pizza and Italian, lovely for dinner.
  • 💵 Soda La Parada — classic, cheap Tico food right on the main square, open late.
  • 💵 Órganico Fortuna — fresh bowls, smoothies and vegetarian/vegan options.
  • 💰 Chifa La Familia Feliz — beloved Peruvian–Chinese, generous portions.
  • 💵 Restaurante Nene's — long-time local favourite for Costa Rican classics.

🏙️ San José

  • ✨ Restaurante Grano de Oro — refined Costa Rican–European in a landmark hotel; save room for the Pié.
  • ✨ Silvestre — modern Costa Rican tasting menu by a celebrated local chef.
  • 💰 Nuestra Tierra — rustic, tourist-friendly Tico food near the centre.
  • 💰 La Esquina de Buenos Aires — beloved Argentine steaks & pasta.
  • 💵 Soda Tapia — a San José institution near La Sabana for casados & batidos.
  • 💰 Kalú Café & Bistro — relaxed brunch, salads and desserts in Barrio Escalante.

☁️ Monteverde

  • 💰 Tramonti — wood-fired pizza & Italian, a local institution.
  • 💰 Café Monteverde — farm-to-table with its own estate coffee.
  • 💵 Sabor Tico — beloved, well-priced traditional Costa Rican food.
  • 💵 Orchid Coffee Shop — great breakfasts, brunch and coffee.
  • 💵 Taco Taco — fresh, casual Mexican.
  • ✨ Sofía — refined Nuevo Latino, one of Monteverde's best dinners.

🏖️ Manuel Antonio / Quepos

  • ✨ El Avión — dinner & drinks inside a converted cargo plane with sunset ocean views.
  • 💰 Ronny's Place — romantic hilltop sunsets and Tico–international dishes.
  • 💰 Barba Roja — a Manuel Antonio classic for drinks, sunsets and dinner.
  • 💵 Agua Azul — casual open-air spot with great burgers and ocean views.
  • 💵 Emilio's Café — excellent breakfasts, coffee and desserts.
  • 💵 Falafel Bar — fresh, affordable Mediterranean in Quepos.
