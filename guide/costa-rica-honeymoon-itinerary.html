Costa Rica Honeymoon Itinerary: 6 Luxury Days

Rainforest romance and Pacific sunsets — three nights at the Arenal Volcano and three on the beaches of Manuel Antonio. Five-star stays (with a value pick too), the best couples' experiences, and private VIP transport so you never lift a finger.

If you want comfort, privacy and unforgettable views, this is the honeymoon route. You'll land in San José (SJO), unwind in the hot springs beneath the Arenal Volcano, then finish with rainforest-meets-ocean sunsets in Manuel Antonio. Every transfer is private and door-to-door — upgrade to Travesía VIP for a driver-guide, scenic stops and a welcome kit.

Prices are per vehicle, not per person — a private car just for the two of you. Taxes and tolls included. Ask us about Travesía VIP for the full experience.

Days 1–3 · Arenal / La Fortuna 🌋

Start with three romantic nights under the volcano — think private hot-spring soaks, waterfalls and rainforest views from your room.

Where to stay

  • ✨ 5-star / romantic: Nayara Springs (adults, private plunge pools), Nayara Gardens, Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa, The Springs Resort & Spa, Arenal Kioro Suites & Spa.
  • 💵 Special for less: Arenal Manoa, Volcano Lodge & Springs, Arenal Springs Resort, Hotel Lomas del Volcán, Casa Luna Lodge & Spa.

Romantic things to do

⭐ Our pick — the Travesía La Fortuna Full-Day Tour: Mistico hanging bridges, a guided volcano hike at El Silencio, tortilla-making, a traditional lunch and the La Fortuna Waterfall — private transport and a private guide, just for you two. See the tour →
  • Private evening at Tabacón hot springs.
  • La Fortuna Waterfall & a couples' swim.
  • El Silencio volcano trail at sunset.
  • Chocolate or coffee tasting for two.

Where to eat

  • Don Rufino — La Fortuna's most popular dinner spot.
  • Anch'io Ristorante — candle-lit wood-fired Italian.
  • Órganico Fortuna — light, fresh lunches.
  • Chifa La Familia Feliz — popular Peruvian–Chinese.
  • Your resort's signature restaurant (Nayara & Tabacón are excellent).
🚐 Getting here: SJO Airport → La Fortuna, ~3h, from $220 per vehicle. See price & book this transfer →

Days 4–6 · Manuel Antonio 🌅

Trade the volcano for the ocean — rainforest that spills onto the beach, monkeys in the trees and famously beautiful sunsets.

Where to stay

  • ✨ 5-star / romantic: Arenas del Mar, Gaia Hotel & Reserve (adults-only), Tulemar Resort, Makanda by the Sea, Parador Resort & Spa.
  • 💵 Special for less: Hotel Plaza Yara, Shana by the Beach, Hotel Costa Verde, Hotel Verde Mar, La Mariposa Hotel.

Romantic things to do

  • Private sunset catamaran with snorkeling & drinks.
  • Guided walk in Manuel Antonio National Park (sloths, monkeys, beaches).
  • Couples' spa day at your resort.
  • Sunset cocktails with a view at Playa Biesanz or a clifftop bar.

Where to eat

  • El Avión — dinner in a converted cargo plane with sunset ocean views.
  • Ronny's Place — romantic hilltop sunsets.
  • Barba Roja — a Manuel Antonio classic for drinks & dinner.
  • Agua Azul — open-air lunch with ocean views.
  • Emilio's Café — breakfasts, coffee & desserts.
🚐 Getting here: La Fortuna → Manuel Antonio, ~5.5h, from $330 per vehicle. See price & book this transfer →
